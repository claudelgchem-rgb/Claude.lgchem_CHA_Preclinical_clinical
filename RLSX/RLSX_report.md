## 2. H0 판정 — supported, partially supported, or rejected

**H0 as posed:** historically the rate-limiting step was research to find hit protein structures and sequences; with AI progress, preclinical and clinical will become rate-limiting. [INFER] The hypothesis is restated here verbatim as the object under test, not as a premise.

**Verdict: PARTIALLY SUPPORTED, and for reasons that damage rather than vindicate the hypothesis.** Its historical half is rejected for the aggregate pipeline on per-programme economics, with two named exceptions and one structural blind spot that cannot be closed. Its forward half is not falsifiable as posed and is therefore not adopted as a finding; restated as a rate claim it is currently supported, and the thing it predicts was already true before AI existed [E-9512 하] [E-9514 하].

### H0-a — was discovery actually the historical rate-limiting step?

**Rejected as a statement about the aggregate pipeline, on per-programme economics only.** Four independent lines converge. Attrition is overwhelmingly clinical: in the only stage model that resolves discovery explicitly, the three discovery stages together retain 51% of projects while Phase II alone retains 34% [E-1018 중], and cumulative probability from target selection is 4.1% against 11.7% from Phase I entry, so about 65% of lifetime attrition occurs after the molecule enters humans [E-1097 중]. Cash is overwhelmingly clinical: pre-human work is 15.9% of PhRMA member R&D outlay against 28.8% for Phase III alone [E-1042 중], 6.8% of cash outlay on Sertkaya's bottom-up model [E-1013 중], and 12.4% median measured directly from company filings [E-1007 중]. Time is majority clinical, at 21–41% pre-IND depending on where the clock starts [E-1093 중]. And the failure census is unambiguous: of nine terminated AI-derived assets with an identifiable failure mode, seven failed on target validity, efficacy, exposure or unpredicted toxicity, and not one failed because the molecule could not be found, designed or synthesised [E-2043 하] [E-2035 하] [E-2039 하] [E-2041 하] [E-2033 하].

**The single cleanest disconfirmation is a natural experiment on the discovery step itself, and it was produced by the red team while arguing the opposite case.** Of 50 first-in-class small molecules approved 1999–2008, 28 (56%) came from phenotypic screening and only 17 (34%) from target-based approaches — the paradigm the genomics expansion was supposed to supercharge produced fewer first-in-class medicines than the one it replaced [E-9505 중]. The pattern persists: novel biological targets entering the pipeline fell from about 100 a year to 30 in 2024 while the overall pipeline doubled and venture investment tripled [E-9509 하]. [INFER] Target supply is not what is scarce; target appetite is.

**The rejection covers per-programme economics only, and the programme-count channel is undetermined.** Every H0-a test in this run is conditioned on a programme having started, and none of them can see programmes suppressed before they began. About 85% of the druggable genome has never yielded an approved drug [E-9536 중], fewer than a tenth of the roughly 4,500 druggable genes carry an approved drug [E-9501 중], and RAS spent 39 years between confirmed oncogene and first direct inhibitor with no programme appearing in the attrition statistics for most of that interval [E-9502 중]. That counterfactual is not recorded anywhere and cannot be closed with evidence, which is precisely why the word "rejected" must never appear here unqualified. [INFER] This is the strongest surviving case for H0-a and this report does not dispose of it.

**A second structural qualification: stage of observation is not stage of cause.** A Phase II efficacy failure is observed in the clinic and is frequently caused at target selection, which is a discovery-stage act [E-9507 중]. Attributing that failure to "clinical" is an artefact of when the failure becomes visible, and the distinction changes which intervention has elasticity: drug mechanisms with human genetic support have a 2.6-fold higher probability of success [E-9507 중] — **2.6×, not the recycled 2×** [E-3100 중] — and genetic evidence already supported 63% of the 428 FDA approvals of 2013–2022 [E-9508 중]. The precedent that a discovery-stage improvement can move the system also exists: attrition attributed to poor pharmacokinetics fell from about 40% of failures in 1991 to about 10% by 2000 [E-9506 하], which shows that discovery improvement **relocates** the constraint rather than relieving it, since safety and efficacy absorbed the freed share.

**The rejection is domain-conditional and two counter-domains are documented.** No major new class of antibiotic was introduced between 1962 and 2000, an interval the field itself labels the discovery void [E-9503 중], and GlaxoSmithKline evaluated more than 300 candidate antibacterial targets across 70 high-throughput screening campaigns for almost no usable chemical matter [E-9504 중]. In those domains chemical matter, not target biology, was the binding scarcity. Any sentence of the form "discovery was never the bottleneck" is too strong and is not written in this report.

**One widely circulated claim about animal-model non-predictivity is removed entirely rather than qualified, and is not restated here in any form.** It has no primary study at origin, and its denominator is misapplied: the headline percentage is a share of the compounds that clear preclinical, not a share of all compounds entering development [E-9554 중] [E-3019 하].

### H0-b — duration and probability of success are separate axes, tested separately

**Axis 1, duration: PARTIALLY SUBSTANTIATED at roughly 30–50% on preclinical activities.** Two peer-reviewed reviews independently report a 30–50% reduction in screening and candidate-selection time [E-2009 중] [E-2010 중], and a third independent review reports the same range from a systematic review of 20 AI-driven programmes 2018–2023 while stating that Phase I/II attrition remained at industry benchmarks [E-2010 중]. This report never states the per-programme saving in years in its own voice, because the "2.5–4.5 years saved" framing exists only in company-issued material with no matched control cohort [E-2030 하] [E-2031 하] [E-9534 하]. The compression does not propagate: median time from company founding to first Phase 1 entry across 63 AI-native companies is 6.5 years [E-2054 하], Isomorphic Labs had zero registered studies on 18 August 2026 [E-2048 중], and clinical development timelines are explicitly reported as essentially unchanged [E-2009 중].

**Axis 2, probability of success: the evidentiary state is INDETERMINATE, not null.** The heading "no evidence" does not stand over a claim about the effect, because the comparator does the work: the AI cohort's Phase II figure of about 40% is +3 points against the historical 37% benchmark and +12 points against the contemporaneous programme-level rate of 28% [E-9537 하] [E-9513 하] [E-2003 중]. No adequately powered, denominator-matched comparison exists in either direction. Detecting a 37%-versus-40% difference at 80% power requires about 4,126 molecules per arm [E-2065 하], against 8 AI-enabled assets that had completed Phase 2 field-wide as of mid-2026 [E-2055 하]. The Wilson 95% interval on an 85% Phase I rate at the recovered denominator of n=24 is 66.1–94.3%, which overlaps conventional Phase I rates [E-2066 중] [E-3008 중]. The terminal outcome measure is empty: **zero FDA approvals of AI-discovered drugs as of 2026** [E-2053 중], after roughly USD 8.9 billion invested in AI-native discovery [E-2053 중].

**What is genuinely substantiated on the computation axis.** AlphaFold-class models produce structures at CASP-level accuracy, with ESMFold at a median TM-score of 0.95 against AlphaFold2's 0.96 [E-2019 중] across more than 200 million predicted structures [E-2018 하]. Prospective docking against unrefined AF2 models matched experimental structures on hit rate — 54% versus 51% at the σ2 receptor and 26% versus 23% at 5-HT2A [E-2012 중] — while retrospective enrichment benchmarks penalise AF2 models against holo structures, with mean top-5% enrichment factors of 2.24 for X-ray, 2.42 for cryo-EM and 1.82 for AF2 across 32 class A GPCRs [E-2015 중]. Pooled independent de novo binder success across 3,766 designs and 15 targets is 11.6% [E-2020 중], against developer self-reports as high as 68% [E-2023 하] — a 5.9-fold spread between the independent pooled figure and the highest self-report. One de novo designed protein has ever entered human testing and it was discontinued in November 2022 [E-2025 하].

**Whose count of AI clinical molecules, and by what rule.** Any statement of how many AI-derived molecules are in the clinic must name its counting rule, because the ledger holds four irreconcilable counts and no record reconciles the inclusion criteria: **29** cumulative AI-derived programmes reaching human studies [E-9007 중], **75+** cumulative by end-2024 [E-2072 하], **117** AI-enabled clinical candidates across 63 companies on a December 2025 data cut [E-2054 하], and an unrecorded aggregator figure of about 175. This report uses 29 where a conservative regulator-adjacent count is needed and 117 only where the broad "AI-enabled" definition is explicitly named.

### H0-c — the Amdahl ceiling, quantified

Setting discovery time and cost to zero removes, at most: **21–41% of total elapsed development time** on the DiMasi, Sertkaya and Paul clocks [E-1093 중] [E-1094 중]; **23–37%** on an independent computation from a 9.1-year clinical span [E-3098 하]; and **33.3%** in the flow model, which resolves discovery as 4.5 years of a 13.5-year programme [E-9039 하]. On cost: **40–43% of capitalized cost** [E-1094 중], **41.0%** in the model on the same free-discovery definition [E-9039 하], **28.1% of risk-adjusted cost** [E-9039 하], but only **7–31% of actual cash outlay** [E-1094 중] and **5.1% of the true cash cost of one successful molecule** in the model [E-9039 하]. On approvals the model returns **0.0%** [E-9039 하], and that figure alone carries the ⚠ LOW-EVIDENCE CLAIM banner.

[INFER] The reason the capitalized ceiling looks large while the cash ceiling looks small is that a capitalized cost is mostly compound interest on early spending and on downstream attrition. Making discovery instantaneous removes the pre-clinical duration from the compounding horizon and does nothing to the attrition the compounding is applied to. That is the H0-b distinction in its sharpest form.

**The clinical side has its own Amdahl ceiling and it is comparable in size.** Perfect, instantaneous, costless recruitment — a physical impossibility — removes between 24.8% and 60.8% of clinical elapsed time, or 14.7% to 36.2% of a 10.5-year programme [E-5071 중], because 18 of a median 29.6-month Phase III is spent recruiting [E-5060 중] [E-5001 중]. Driving FDA review to zero removes at most about 5–8% of total elapsed development time [E-4057 중]. [INFER] No single stage removal, on either side of the IND, takes a decade-long programme below about eight years.

### H0-d — "preclinical/clinical" is not one step

Decomposed against the catalogue, "preclinical/clinical" splits into a **probability tier that discovery compression does not relieve at all** and a **capacity tier that shifts**. The probability tier is B1 target validation [E-3012 중], B8 toxicity [E-3045 중], B17 clinical development design [E-9541 하] and B2 translation [E-3017 중]; none of these is touched by making discovery faster [E-9031 하]. The capacity tier is B9's IND-enabling non-human-primate toxicology fragment, where up to two-thirds of primate requests have been unfillable since 2021 [E-3050 하]; B6's shared manufacturing inputs [E-6064 하]; and B3/B4 matched clinical site capacity [E-5069 중]. Regulatory review (B5) does not enter either tier: the pending FY2024 pile of 1,690 submissions was inside goal dates rather than overdue [E-4021 중], IND intake grows at 1.7% a year and decelerating [E-4025 중], and CBER built cell-and-gene review capacity that submissions did not fill, running 7–8 approvals a year against FDA's own projection of 10–20 [E-4050 하] [E-4052 중].

**The forward half of H0 is a restatement of a forty-year status quo, not a prediction, and is not adopted in rank terms.** If clinical stages have dominated time, cost and attrition for the entire period for which stage-resolved data exist, then asserting that clinical will become rate-limiting asserts that something already true will remain true [E-1018 중] [E-1042 중] [E-1093 중]. Restated as a falsifiable rate claim it has content and is currently supported: cumulative likelihood of approval from Phase I fell across successive analyses of the same dataset from 10.4% (2014) to 9.6% (2016), 7.9% (2021) and 6.7% (2024) [E-9512 하], while the share of trials ending prematurely fell from 10.6% in 2010 to 4.7% in 2021 [E-9514 하], so the decline is not an artefact of operational failure. **The observation that would falsify it is any scenario in which clinical probability of success stabilises or rises through 2030.**

### 3.3 The T axis — where elapsed time actually goes
